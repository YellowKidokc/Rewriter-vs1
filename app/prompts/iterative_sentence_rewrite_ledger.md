# Iterative Sentence Rewrite Ledger Prompt

Review only the active sentence in its updated draft context. Generate no more than three alternatives and keep every theological, mathematical, data, name, date, citation, and key-term commitment unchanged unless the source sentence already supports the change.

For each candidate, report:
- clarity gain
- structural fit
- claim accuracy
- meaning-drift risk
- claim-strength risk
- reason for the score

If meaning or claim risk is uncertain, mark the sentence as `Needs David Review` instead of silently rewriting it.
